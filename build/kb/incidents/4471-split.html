hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Same flipped date, different bug: the fix is the hint (or the form’s reading), not the parser. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3.

one-day. No flip at all.
