parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out flipped: booked 7 April, invite on 4 July. It hits app, email and booking-v2 form reports. Fixed when #4471 closed.

hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Same flipped date, different bug: the fix is the hint (or the form’s reading), not the parser. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired.

one-day. No flip at all. A date lands one day early: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “every booking in June is one day off”, “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”, “the invite arrived before I made the booking”. That points at time zones or the clock change, not date order. The desk files them under parser by tenant, so they are counted there too. Still open.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and ten of the twelve subjects in #4471 are shared across problems (“Month and day swapped?” covers all three). The subject says what the reporter saw, not what broke. Intake’s tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, while the cause depends on the tenant and its form. Both 0001 and the triage page were last reviewed in June 2024.

What to do

  • Engineering: the one-day ticket is still open. Look at time zones and the clock change in invite dates.
  • Desk: when a report shows a date moved by one day, not swapped, it is not #4471 and it is not fixed.
  • Decision 0001 needs a new record: merge on cause (tenant, form, channel), not subject.
